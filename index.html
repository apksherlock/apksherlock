What I can do for you

Not sure which one you need? Describe your problem and I’ll tell you honestly what makes sense — and what doesn’t.

  • Complete mobile apps

    New apps for Android and iPhone, plus the server behind them — user accounts, data, payments, notifications and admin panels. One person responsible for the whole thing, from a simple customer app to large company software.

  • Fixing, maintaining and publishing apps

    Your app crashes, is slow, or the original developer is gone? I take over, fix the problems and keep it up to date. I also get apps into Google Play and the App Store — and sort out rejections, policy warnings and suspended listings.

  • App security check

    I look at your Android app the way an attacker would and find the weak spots before someone else does. Your developers get a detailed report: what’s at risk, how serious it is and exactly how to fix it.

  • Is this phone or app infected?

    You suspect an Android phone or app is spying on you or behaving strangely — company or personal? I check it for malware and tell you what I found and what to do. Android only.

  • Automation and AI

    Let software do the repetitive work: chat bots for Telegram or WhatsApp, AI assistants that answer customer questions, automatic reports and reminders, and links between the tools you already use.

  • Websites

    Clean, fast websites that explain what you offer and make it easy for customers to get in touch.

  • Technical partner for founders

    I help you plan the product, choose the right technology and avoid expensive mistakes — before a single line of code is written.

  • Training for developer teams

    Hands-on coaching for teams moving into Android or modern app development.

About me

I’m Stavro, a software engineer who has spent the last 9 years building Android apps — from small Albanian startups to the screens inside BMW cars. Along the way I’ve led teams, worked side by side with designers and product owners, and seen what makes software projects succeed or fail.

My home turf is native Android, but I build the whole product — the app and the server behind it — and I’m not tied to one tool: I pick whatever fits your project, your budget and your plans. I also research mobile security in my spare time, which makes the apps I build safer from the start.

I work remotely with clients worldwide, in English, German or Albanian.
